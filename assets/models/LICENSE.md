Models by Quaternius (https://quaternius.com), released under CC0 1.0 (public
domain), from these packs:

- Ultimate Space Kit (files prefixed `usk_`): ship, astronauts, mechs, alien
  trees, plants, rocks and enemies
- Ultimate Nature Pack: trees, bushes, grass, flowers, cacti, rocks
- Farm Animals, Animal Pack Vol. 2, Animated Fish Pack, Cute Animated Monsters

FBX sources were converted to glTF with three.js (`tools/convert-models.mjs`)
and compressed with gltfpack (meshopt). No changes to the artwork itself.

Files prefixed `ph_` are photoscanned models from Poly Haven
(https://polyhaven.com), CC0 1.0: quiver_tree_01/02, fern_02, shrub_03/04,
flower_gazania, periwinkle_plant, dandelion_01, celandine_01, rock_moss_set_01/02,
boulder_01, namaqualand_boulder_02. Geometry was simplified with gltfpack
(`*_lod` files are further simplified distant versions) and textures resized to
512 px.
